A project exploring the updates, trends, and evolution of computer life in our modern world.
# World Update: The Silicon Fragility

## The Reality
We've built our entire modern civilization on top of computer infrastructure. But tech life has a fatal flaw: **it is entirely dependent on electricity.

## The Weakness
* No Power = No Systems: Servers, networks, AI, and code are completely useless without continuous power. 
* Single Point of Failure: A major grid breakdown instantly freezes the digital world. 

## The Shift
We need to stop over-relying on computers to sustain our lives. True security comes from human empowerment, offline resilience, and independent skills that don't plug into a wall.
